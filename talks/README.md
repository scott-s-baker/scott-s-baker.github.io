# Videos & Talks

Keynotes, webinars, and analyst/press appearances — the external-authority side of your brand.

**How to use this folder:**
- Videos should be **hosted** (YouTube, Vimeo, the event's site) — don't upload large video files to the repo. Just link to them.
- For each talk, capture: title, venue/date, a one-line description, and the video URL. Optionally add a thumbnail image to `../assets/`.
- To feature on the homepage, edit the cards in the `#talks` section of `../index.html` and set each link to the video URL.

**Good candidates:**
- The Cyber Resiliency webinar you're hosting
- Conference keynotes
- Analyst or press interview clips
