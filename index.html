About

Engineer turned marketer; I build the teams, standards, and systems that scale great marketing.

I define categories and build the narrative, positioning, launch strategy, press- and analyst-relations, and sales-enablement engines that move markets and revenue. The throughline across every role: take an underdeveloped marketing or product-marketing function and build it into the engine other teams model themselves on.

I reignited a stagnant multi-billion-dollar portfolio to 52% above revenue targets, moved portfolios to #1 positions in recognized analyst publications, drove campaigns responsible for delivering $6B USD in revenue (product-led growth, person-led growth, solution-led growth, and account-based marketing programs), and grew partner revenue 240% in 18 months. Because I started my career as a software engineer (real-time systems at NASA, with enterprise data platform and AI-infrastructure patents), I translate deeply technical products into value propositions that both technical and business buyers act on, and I lead AI-native every day.
