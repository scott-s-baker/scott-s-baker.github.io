Product Marketing RACI Matrix

How I define and operationalize the role of Product Marketing — mapping ~70 deliverables across the full go-to-market org (Marketing, Product, Enablement, Sales) to clear accountability. This is how I remove ambiguity, end turf wars, and make a PMM team the connective tissue of GTM.

🧭 Illustrative sample of a role-definition framework I authored. Roles are functional (no individuals named); a few internal tool names have been generalized.
79 deliverables × 30 functions
A Accountable — Final decision-making authority — one per task.R Responsible — Assigned to complete the deliverable.D Driver — Actively drives and partners with the Accountable role.S Support — Provides support during execution.C Consulted — Adviser / SME consulted before a decision.I Informed — Kept informed after a decision or action.
