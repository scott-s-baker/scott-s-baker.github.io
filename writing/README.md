# Writing & Thought Leadership

Put your writing samples here — the pieces that show your voice and how you turn complexity into a clear narrative.

**How to use this folder:**
- Add each article as a Markdown file (e.g. `category-led-repositioning.md`) or a PDF.
- Or, if a piece lives on LinkedIn/Medium/a company blog, skip the file and just link to it from a card in `../index.html`.
- To feature a piece on the homepage, edit the matching `<div class="card">` in `index.html` and point its link here (e.g. `writing/category-led-repositioning.md`) or to the external URL.

**Good candidates to include:**
- A positioning or category essay
- A market POV on AI in your space
- A strong LinkedIn post or byline
- An excerpt/adaptation from *Intelligent Data Governance for Dummies*
